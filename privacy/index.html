Privacy

BridgeX Privacy Policy
Updated November 6, 2026

Developer: Viacheslav Kuzmin.
Privacy questions and requests to delete information sent to support: support@techdatacloud.org.

BridgeX turns an iPhone, iPad, Android phone or tablet into a remote mouse and keyboard. Computer companions include Mac and a Windows Wi-Fi beta. Compatibility depends on the app and companion versions; see https://bridgexapp.org/download/ for current requirements and limitations. Remote control requires no BridgeX account or developer server. BridgeX has no ads, tracking or analytics SDKs. The developer does not automatically receive commands, typed text, device names, pairing keys or session history.

Local connections

Commands are encrypted and sent directly between your mobile device and paired computer over the supported local network or Bluetooth connection. Windows Bluetooth is not supported in the current public beta. Local or nearby discovery can announce a computer name and service availability. Pairing is required to control a computer. Information entered into another app is subsequently handled by that app under its own policies.

Pairing and storage

Pairing records include random device identifiers, names, credentials and trusted-device information. Apple apps protect pairing secrets in Keychain and store preferences locally in UserDefaults. Android stores preferences in app-private storage and encrypts pairing secrets with an Android Keystore key; backup and transfer of these records are disabled. Windows protects identity and pairing secrets with Windows DPAPI and stores preferences locally. No advertising identifier is used.

Typed text and preview

BridgeX does not save typing sessions to its files or send them to the developer. While preview is enabled, temporary in-memory session context allows Backspace to refill the visible tail. The preview shows up to the last 512 characters on Apple devices or 512 Unicode code points on Android. Closing the keyboard, disconnecting, losing control permission or leaving the app for the background clears its local typing context. Clearing or hiding the preview erases preview history. BridgeX does not read an existing computer field or record the computer screen.

Clipboard and paste

Where supported, explicitly choosing Paste can read text from the mobile device clipboard and send it as typed text to the paired computer. Copy/Paste shortcuts for the computer act on the computer's clipboard. BridgeX does not monitor or automatically synchronize clipboards. The operating system and your selected keyboard may manage their own clipboard history under their own policies.

Permissions

Camera access is optional for scanning pairing QR codes locally; frames are not saved or uploaded. Manual pairing is also available. Bluetooth/Nearby devices and local-network access enable discovery and connections, not location tracking. Mac Accessibility permission permits remote mouse and keyboard input. Android does not use an Accessibility service. You can revoke system permissions in device settings.

Deletion and revocation

Use Forget in the mobile app's computer list to remove a saved pairing, and Revoke access on the computer to remove the mobile device's trust record and close its connection. Do both to remove pairing data from both sides. Apple Keychain records can remain after uninstalling. Android uninstall removes its local app data but does not revoke the computer's trust record. The Windows uninstaller lets you choose whether to keep pairing data.

Support and external pages

BridgeX does not automatically upload diagnostic reports. Information you voluntarily send to support is used to respond; request deletion at support@techdatacloud.org. Do not send pairing codes, keys or passwords. Opening the website or support links uses your chosen browser or mail app. The website and hosting provider may receive ordinary HTTP request metadata; BridgeX does not send remote-control session contents to them.

Children and families

BridgeX is intended for computer users aged 6 and above, including families. It does not ask for a child's name, age, email, location or advertising identifier. There are no ads, purchases, public profiles, chat or content feeds. A parent or guardian should supervise pairing and content opened on the computer and may request deletion of support correspondence.

Keyboards, stores and device services

Your selected keyboard processes input under its own policy. BridgeX requests no personalized learning for the Android input field but cannot guarantee another keyboard's behavior. Apple, Google Play and operating-system services may process store, installation, licensing and diagnostic information under their own policies and provide the developer with aggregate technical reports or feedback you choose to send. BridgeX does not send them typed text, pairing keys or remote-control sessions.
