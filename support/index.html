LET'S GET YOU CONNECTED

BridgeX support

A question or a problem? Email support@techdatacloud.org.

First connection: phone or tablet → PC/Mac

Install BridgeX on your phone or tablet and the matching companion on your computer. Either installation order works. Open both apps. Mac supports home Wi-Fi or direct Bluetooth; the Windows beta uses Wi-Fi. Generate a QR code on the computer and scan it in BridgeX on the phone or tablet. The code is single-use and expires after two minutes.

Computer not found

Keep the computer on and signed in, with BridgeX running. For Wi-Fi, use the same home network; a computer connected to the router by Ethernet also works. Guest-network isolation or a VPN that blocks local traffic can prevent discovery. On Mac you can choose Bluetooth instead. Windows Bluetooth is not confirmed in the public beta.

Connected, but the cursor will not move

On Mac, enable BridgeX in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. If it is missing, add BridgeX from Applications. On Windows, open a regular app in your signed-in session. The beta cannot control the sign-in screen, UAC prompts or apps running as administrator.

Move and resize windows

Position the cursor on the title bar or corner. Tap Hold left, move on the trackpad, then tap Release. Alternatively, hold Left click with one finger and move with another. Pressure-sensitive hardware is not required.

Scrolling goes the wrong way

Open BridgeX settings on your phone or tablet and change Invert scrolling. The choice also changes inertia direction and is saved for the next launch.

Remove a saved computer or revoke a phone

Newer mobile builds have a Computers list: choose Forget computer for the entry you want to remove. This removes only the mobile copy of the pairing. To revoke the phone’s access, also remove it from the trusted-device list in BridgeX on the computer. Other saved computers are kept.

What to include in a support email

Include phone/tablet and computer models, operating-system and BridgeX versions on both devices, the selected connection method and steps to reproduce. Use an error screenshot only if it contains no private data. Do not send pairing codes or QR images, keys, passwords or private typed text.

Windows beta: connection and limits

Run the signed installer and open BridgeX from Start. Use the same home network and choose Wi-Fi on the phone. Allow BridgeX local-network access when prompted. Keep the PC on and signed in. Bluetooth is not confirmed; login, UAC and elevated apps are unsupported. Typing may lag or contain incorrect characters, especially with long text. Check the result and send feedback.

Camera denied or QR code expired

Use manual code entry in the mobile pairing screen if you cannot use the camera. Copy the current connection code from BridgeX on your computer and transfer it privately. An expired or already used code needs a fresh QR code from the computer. Keep connection codes out of screenshots and support messages.

Phone permissions

On iPhone/iPad, review BridgeX under Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network for Wi-Fi and Bluetooth for Bluetooth. On Android, open Settings → Apps → BridgeX → Permissions and review Nearby devices for Bluetooth and Camera for QR scanning. Menu names vary by device. Camera access is optional when you enter a code manually.

Three-finger drag and system gestures

With three-finger drag enabled, three fingers move or resize a window; use four fingers for system swipes. With it disabled, three-finger swipes control windows and desktops. Available actions depend on Mac or Windows. Pinch zoom uses app keyboard shortcuts, so the foreground app must support them.

Typing preview and TV controls

The typing row shows text entered on the phone, not the contents of the computer’s field. Hide or clear it without deleting text already sent; closing the keyboard clears the local draft. For TV seeking and fullscreen, select the video and leave text fields first. HDMI volume may need the television’s own remote.
