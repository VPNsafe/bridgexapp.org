LET'S GET YOU CONNECTED

BridgeX support

A question or a problem? Email support@techdatacloud.org.

First connection

Install and open BridgeX on your Mac. Grant Accessibility permission, enable Bluetooth or join the same home network, then generate a QR code on the Mac and scan it with BridgeX on your iPhone or Android phone. The code is single-use and expires after two minutes.

Mac not found

Check that your Mac is on and BridgeX is running. In the iPhone app settings, choose Connection → Bluetooth. Wi-Fi requires both devices on the same network; guest networks may isolate devices.

Connected, but the cursor won't move

Check the Accessibility permission for BridgeX on your Mac. If you just granted it, restart the Mac companion and wait for your phone to reconnect.

Move and resize windows

Position the cursor on the title bar or corner. Tap Hold left, move on the trackpad, then tap Release. Alternatively, hold Left click with one finger and move with another.

Scrolling goes the wrong way

Open BridgeX settings on your iPhone or Android phone and change Invert scrolling. This also changes the direction of inertia.

Revoke access

On the Mac, choose Revoke access next to your phone. On the iPhone, open settings and choose Forget Mac. Do both to remove the pairing from both devices.

What to include in a support email

Tell us your device models, iOS and macOS versions, BridgeX version, connection type and steps to reproduce the problem. An error screenshot can help. Do not send pairing QR codes, keys, passwords or private typed text.

Windows beta: connection and limits

Run the installer and open BridgeX from the Start menu. Connect the phone and PC to the same home network. Allow BridgeX local-network access when prompted; choose Wi-Fi in the phone app. Generate a QR code in BridgeX on the PC and scan it using BridgeX on your phone. Keep the PC on and signed in. Beta limits: Bluetooth is not confirmed. Login screens, UAC and elevated apps are not supported. Typing can lag or occasionally produce incorrect characters on some systems, especially with long text. Check your input and send feedback to support.
