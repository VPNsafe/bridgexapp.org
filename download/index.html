THE OTHER HALF OF YOUR REMOTE

Your Mac.
Ready to connect.

The Mac companion receives commands from your iPhone. It lives in the menu bar, connects over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi and stays out of your way.

Preparing for release

Free · macOS 14+ · Apple Silicon and Intel

INSTALL ON YOUR MAC

  1. Move it to Applications

    Open the disk image and drag BridgeX into the Applications folder.

  2. Allow control

    Open BridgeX and grant permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

  3. Connect your iPhone

    Generate a QR code on your Mac and scan it using BridgeX on your phone.
