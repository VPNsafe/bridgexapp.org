SET IT UP ONCE

Your phone and computer.
Three simple steps.

  1. Install the computer companion

    Download BridgeX for Mac or Windows. On Mac, grant Accessibility permission; on Windows, allow BridgeX local-network access when prompted.

  2. Choose your connection

    Connect both devices to the same home network. Mac also supports direct Bluetooth.

  3. Scan the QR code

    Show a QR code in BridgeX on your computer and scan it with BridgeX on iPhone or Android. Either app can be installed first.

Setup guide and help ↗

BEFORE YOU CONNECT

Any questions?

Do I need the internet?

Only for downloading the apps. Control works over your local Wi-Fi network; Mac also supports direct Bluetooth. The Windows beta currently uses Wi-Fi.

What do I install on the computer?

The free BridgeX companion for macOS 14+ on Apple silicon, or the public beta for Windows 11 x64. The phone app is a separate install.

Can I control a Mac that's switched off?

Your Mac must be on, signed in and running BridgeX. Wake-up and control before login are not supported.

Do I need an account?

No. Pair your devices with a QR code. The developer does not receive your typed text or control history.

Is it free?

Yes. The first version is free, with no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads.

Are the gestures identical to a Mac trackpad?

BridgeX supports scrolling, pinch zoom and system gestures through standard keyboard shortcuts. Behaviour depends on the app and Mac settings. Continuous hardware magnification and Force Touch are not emulated.
